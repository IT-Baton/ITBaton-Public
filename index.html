How to play

  1. Each level gives you a set of letters, from 3 up to 7.
  2. The grid hides words that cross like a crossword.
  3. Connect letters to spell a word. A correct word is revealed on the grid.
  4. You cannot reuse the same letter tile twice in one word. A level can still give you more than one copy of a letter.
  5. Uncover every word to finish the level. Later levels add letters and words, and easier boards return so you can catch your breath.

Hints, emeralds, and rewards

Finishing a level earns emeralds, the in-game currency. Spend them on optional hints: reveal a random letter on the grid, or choose the letter you want. Some players barely use hints. Others spend them on a stubborn crossing.

Progress milestones add extra emeralds, boosters, and postcards that customize the board. The milestones stay close together so the next reward is never far away.

Questions

Can I play Word Atlas without installing it?

Yes. Use Play Online to open the browser version. The same puzzle is also a free Android app on Google Play.

Is Word Atlas free?

Word Atlas is free to play. The Android app includes ads and optional in-app purchases for hints. Every level can be finished without spending.

Does Word Atlas have a timer?

No. There is no clock. You can leave a puzzle and come back, and you cannot fail a level.

Who is Word Atlas for?

It is a family word puzzle rated PEGI 3. It fits crossword fans and anyone who wants a calm word game without pressure.

How do hints work?

Levels pay emeralds. Emeralds buy optional hints that reveal a random letter, or a letter you pick. Hints are never required to clear a board.

The App Store version is coming soon. You can play in the browser now, or download it for Android on Google Play.
